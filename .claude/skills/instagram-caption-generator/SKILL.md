## 2026 Algorithm Priorities (READ FIRST)

Meta's 2026 ranking signals (highest → lowest weight):
1. **Sends per Reach** (#1 — DM shares to friends)
2. **Saves** (algorithm loves save-worthy)
3. **Watch-through rate** (Reels: 3-sec hold, completion %)
4. **Comments** (quality — replies + long threads)
5. **Shares** (Story re-shares)
6. Likes (now weakest signal — near-irrelevant)

**Implications:**
- Captions must design for **saves + DM-shares**, not likes
- First **125 characters** matter most (before "...mehr anzeigen" cutoff)
- **Caption-SEO** is now primary discovery driver (Meta indexes captions as search text) — keywords in first line, not hashtag stuffing
- **Hashtags capped at 3-5 max** (IG de-prioritizes posts with >5 hashtags since Dec 2025)
- DM-Share CTAs ("Send this to a friend who...") beat Follow CTAs 3x

## Post-Type Specs

### Feed Image
- Length: 80-200 chars optimal (short = more reach 2026)
- Structure: **Hook with keyword** → 1 insight → 1 CTA
- Hashtags: 3-5 in **first comment** (not caption)
- First-comment hashtags preserve caption-SEO signal

### Carousel (1080x1350 portrait — 3.4x more saves than square)
- Length: 400-700 chars (longer carousels outperform for saves 2026)
- Structure: Promise → Slide tease → Value nuggets → Save CTA
- Slide 1 hook: 80% see it, only 40% reach slide 5 — make slide 1 critical
- Hashtags: 3-5 in first comment
- **Modern language:** NOT "Swipe to see" → use "Slide 5 is the payoff" / "Save this for when you..."

### Reels
- Length: 50-125 chars (muted-view friendly — works without audio)
- Structure: Hook (worked in first line) → 1 sentence value → Save/Share CTA
- **Don't duplicate on-screen text** — caption complements video
- 3-second rule: hook must land in 3 sec (80% drop-off after)
- Hashtags: 3-5 in first comment

### Threads (500 char limit)
- Length: 280-500 chars
- Structure: Hot take → Build → Question/CTA
- **No hashtags** (Threads de-prioritizes them)
- Voice: conversational, opinion-led, punchy
- Example: "Painted with AI for 2 weeks. Result: 40% faster. Soul: intact. Here's the counterintuitive part — the AI killed my bad habits, not my voice. Ask me anything."

### IG Notes (60 char limit)
- One-liner, authentic moment
- No hashtags, no CTAs
- Example: "Studio at 3am. The disco ball is watching me back."

### Story
- Length: max 100 chars (screen real-estate)
- Structure: Raw moment + optional poll/question sticker
- Use poll/question stickers for engagement (count as "saves")

## Hook Patterns (first 125 chars)

All hooks must contain **keywords for SEO** (not just clickbait).

**Specificity (highest save rate 2026):**
- "[Number] [unit]. [Unexpected result]."

**Counterintuitive:**

- "Unreleased: [thing]. Here's why I didn't post it."
- "Before/after: [process]."

**Direct value:**
- "[What you'll learn] in 30 seconds."
- "How to [thing] without [common mistake]."
- "The 3 tools that replaced [thing]."

**Invitation:**
- "If you're making [niche thing], steal this."
- "For the [specific audience] — this one's for you."

## Caption-SEO Rules (2026)

Caption-SEO replaced hashtag-stuffing. Rules:

1. **Keyword in first 10 words** — Meta indexes this heaviest
2. **Natural phrasing, not keyword spam** — "painting process" ✓, "#painting #process" ✗
3. **Alt-text always** — Include accessibility description (Meta's search uses alt-text too)
4. **Location tag** (if relevant): your city/region
5. **@mention collaborators** in caption, not just tag
6. **Match profile bio keywords** to caption topics (topical authority)
