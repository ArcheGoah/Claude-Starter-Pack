# Meta Description Generator

Write meta descriptions that win clicks in search results.

## What Meta Descriptions Do

**They don't directly affect rankings**, but they affect click-through rate (CTR).

Higher CTR → More traffic → Better rankings

**They appear:**
- Google search results (desktop & mobile)
- Social media shares (if no OG description)
- Bookmark previews

## Character Limits

**Mobile:** ~120 characters (most restrictive)  
**Desktop:** ~155-160 characters

**Safest:** Stay under 155 characters

Google truncates with "..." if too long.

## Input Requirements

**Essential:**
- **Page content** - What the page is about
- **Target keyword** - Primary SEO term
- **Search intent** - What users want

**Valuable:**
- **Unique value** - Why click this vs. competitors
- **Page type** - Homepage, blog, product, category
- **Brand voice** - Tone and style

## Meta Description Formula

```
[Target Keyword] + [Benefit/Value] + [CTA or Hook]
```

**Keep it:**
- Under 155 characters
- Including target keyword
- Action-oriented
- Benefit-focused
- Unique per page

## Formula Variations

### Benefit-First
"[Benefit]. [How]. [Why choose us]."

**Example:**
"Learn email marketing that converts. Step-by-step guides, templates, and real examples. Free for beginners."

### Question-Answer
"[Question]? [Answer with keyword]. [Supporting detail]."

**Example:**
"Need project management software? Asana helps teams organize work and hit deadlines. Try free for 30 days."

### Problem-Solution
"[Problem]? [Our solution with keyword]. [Result or proof]."

**Example:**
"Struggling with SEO? Our complete guide covers everything from keywords to backlinks. Used by 10,000+ marketers."

### Feature-Benefit
"[Product/Service] with [key features]. [Main benefit]. [Trust signal]."

**Example:**
"CRM software with pipeline management, email automation, and analytics. Close more deals. Trusted by 5,000+ teams."

## Meta Descriptions by Page Type

### Homepage
Focus on brand value and main offering.

**Example:**
"Acme helps small businesses automate their workflows. No code required. Start free. Join 50,000+ companies saving 10+ hours per week."

### Blog Post
Include main topic, takeaway, and keyword.

**Example:**
"Discover 7 proven email subject lines that boost open rates by 40%. Real examples and A/B test results included. Free templates."

### Product Page
Highlight key features, benefits, and CTA.

**Example:**
"Wireless noise-cancelling headphones with 30-hour battery life. Studio-quality sound. Free shipping. 30-day returns."

### Category Page
Describe what's in the category and why browse.

**Example:**
"Shop 200+ organic skincare products. Clean ingredients. Cruelty-free. Free samples with every order. Ships within 24 hours."

### Service Page
Explain service, benefit, and why choose you.

**Example:**
"Professional website design for small businesses. Custom designs starting at $2,500. Launch in 2 weeks. 500+ happy clients."

### About Page
Share mission, differentiation, and story.

**Example:**
"Founded in 2015, we're on a mission to make healthy eating accessible to everyone. Meet our team and learn our story."

## Power Words for CTR

**Curiosity:** Discover, Learn, Find Out, Revealed, Secret  
**Value:** Free, Best, Ultimate, Complete, Proven  
**Ease:** Simple, Easy, Quick, Step-by-Step, No Code  
**Results:** Boost, Increase, Improve, Double, Maximize  
**Social:** Trusted, Popular, Top-Rated, #1, Award-Winning  
**Urgency:** Today, Now, Limited, Last Chance (use sparingly)

## Including Keywords

**Do:** Include target keyword naturally

"Learn **content marketing** strategies that drive traffic. Free guides, templates, and case studies."

**Don't:** Keyword stuff

"**Content marketing content marketing** tips for **content marketing** success and **content marketing** strategies."

## What to Avoid

❌ **Duplicate descriptions** across pages  
❌ **Too vague:** "Learn more about our products"  
❌ **Keyword stuffing:** Repeating keywords unnaturally  
❌ **Over 160 characters:** Gets truncated  
❌ **Missing value:** Doesn't explain why to click  
❌ **Clickbait:** Promises not delivered on page  
❌ **Passive voice:** "Services can be provided..."  
❌ **No CTA:** Missing action prompt

## Mobile vs. Desktop Optimization

**Mobile (120 chars):**
Prioritize most important info first.

"Learn email marketing. Free guides, templates, case studies. 10K+ students."

**Desktop (155 chars):**
Can add more detail.

"Learn email marketing that actually converts. Complete guides, customizable templates, and real case studies. Join 10,000+ marketers growing their lists."

## A/B Testing Strategy

**Test these variables:**
- Question vs. statement
- Benefit vs. feature focus
- With vs. without numbers
- Short (90 chars) vs. long (155 chars)
- With vs. without CTA

**Monitor:**
- Click-through rate in Google Search Console
- Time on page (did it attract right visitors?)
- Bounce rate (did it set right expectations?)

## Quality Checklist

✅ Under 155 characters  
✅ Includes target keyword naturally  
✅ Describes page content accurately  
✅ Includes clear benefit or value  
✅ Has call-to-action  
✅ Unique (not duplicate)  
✅ Compelling (beats competitors)  
✅ Matches search intent  
✅ No clickbait  
✅ Mobile-friendly (under 120 chars ideal)  

## Output Format

Provide:
1. **Primary meta description** (155 chars max)
2. **Mobile-optimized version** (120 chars max)
3. **Alternative option** (different angle)
4. **Character count** for each
5. "Optimized for [search intent] targeting [keyword]"
